HMEC is working to accelerate marine energy development in Hawaii and across the Pacific region. Achieving that goal requires more than research, projects, and partnerships—it requires a way to capture knowledge, share lessons learned, and make valuable resources accessible to the people who need them. As the marine energy community continues to grow, HMEC is building not only a website — visit us at [hmec.us](https://www.hmec.us/) — but a shared knowledge hub that helps connect information, people, and opportunities.

# MkDocs

[MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/) provides a simple, powerful way to organize information, document processes, and share knowledge across projects, partners, and communities. Much of the information we need already exists; our goal is to collect it in one place, connect it, and make it easier to discover and use.

By adopting a common documentation framework, HMEC can establish consistent standards for communication, instructions, project documentation, and technical resources. This helps create a clear experience for readers while making it easier for contributors to add and maintain content over time.

Online documenation also supports self-guided learning. Whether someone is exploring marine energy for the first time or looking for detailed technical guidance, documentation can provide structured pathways that allow users to learn at their own pace and build their own path forward.

Because MkDocs integrates naturally with GitHub, documentation can evolve alongside projects, datasets, software, and research outputs. Changes are transparent, version-controlled, and easy to review, creating a living knowledge base rather than a collection of static reports.

Most importantly, good documentation preserves institutional knowledge. It reduces duplicated effort, improves onboarding, strengthens collaboration across islands and organizations, and ensures that lessons learned remain accessible long after individual projects are completed.

For HMEC, MkDocs is a practical tool for creating beautiful, searchable documentation—but more importantly, it is a foundation for knowledge sharing, collaboration, and long-term growth within the marine energy community.
